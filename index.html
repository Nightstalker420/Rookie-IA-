The Internal Affairs Group (IAG) conducts impartial, objective administrative and criminal investigations into alleged misconduct involving personnel within the Rook Los Angeles jurisdiction.

Bureau Organization

Administrative Investigation Division (AID)

Responsible for investigating non-criminal complaints regarding departmental policy breaches, procedural failures, and administrative misconduct.

Criminal Investigation Division (CID)

Handles allegations of potential criminal activity committed by sworn or civilian personnel, coordinating directly with local prosecutors.

Force Investigation Division (FID)

Investigates all Categorical Use of Force incidents, officer-involved shootings (OIS), and critical in-custody incidents.
